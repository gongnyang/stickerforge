# Mono ink

Contents: prompt fragment, fits, cautions

## Prompt fragment

Single-color ink drawing: one dark ink color on transparent background, confident outline of even weight, simple hatching for depth, no gradients, no fills except solid dark shapes. Character features simplified but recognizable from the reference. Centered, margin around the shape, no frame, no extra letters.

## Fits well

- Characters with a clear silhouette: cats, birds, round creatures.
- Print on kraft, white, or colored stock; one-color print jobs.
- Packs that need a calm, uniform look.

## Cautions

- Fine hatching disappears at small sizes. Keep lines thick enough for 30 mm prints.
- A transparent image with dark ink looks black on dark backgrounds; check on both.
- The reference colors are dropped by design. Say so at check A.
