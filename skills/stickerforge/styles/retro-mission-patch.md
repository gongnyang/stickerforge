# Retro mission patch

Contents: prompt fragment, fits, cautions

## Prompt fragment

Embroidered patch look: circular or shield-shaped badge border, thick outline, limited palette of four to six flat colors, stitched edge suggested by a ring of short dashes, simple emblem composition with the character at the center. Short text allowed only in a banner, one to three words. Transparent background around the badge, margin around the shape, no extra letters.

## Fits well

- Work-feeling and reaction concepts framed as mission or badge themes.
- Print on sheets; the badge shape also gives an easy die-cut path.
- Small packs of 10 to 30 with a collectible feel.

## Cautions

- Text banners fail more often than free text. Use few words or none.
- Fine stitch marks make busy edges; keep dashes bold.
- A badge makes the character small inside it; check the pilot at the smallest print size.
