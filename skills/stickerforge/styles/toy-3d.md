# Toy 3D

Contents: prompt fragment, fits, cautions

## Prompt fragment

Soft 3D toy render of the character: smooth rounded forms like vinyl or plush material, simple studio-style lighting from the upper left, gentle contact shading on the form only, no ground shadow, no cast shadow outside the shape. Key colors and features from the reference. Centered, margin around the shape, transparent background, no frame, no extra letters.

## Fits well

- Characters that read well as objects: round animals, mascots with simple volumes.
- Subject mix concepts with props that look like toys.
- Packs where a glossy, tactile look is wanted.

## Cautions

- Shadows and glow leave halos on transparent output; the edge halo gate may fail often.
- Shading changes between stickers; compare in the pilot sheet.
- Heavier files and slower generation than flat styles.
- Pose variety is lower; plan more distinct props.
