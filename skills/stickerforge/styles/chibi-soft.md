# Chibi soft

Contents: prompt fragment, fits, cautions

## Prompt fragment

Soft chibi proportions: large head, small body, short limbs, big simple eyes, rounded shapes, gentle pastel-leaning palette drawn from the reference, thin soft outline in a darker tone of the fill color, light flat shading. Warm friendly expressions. Centered, margin around the shape, transparent background, no frame, no extra letters.

## Fits well

- Cute characters for chat and gift stickers.
- Expressive reactions (thanks, sorry, sleepy) where the face carries the sticker.
- Characters that already have round forms.

## Cautions

- Do not copy the look of any existing franchise; describe shapes and palette only.
- Pastel on white paper has low contrast; the white border helps.
- Proportion changes the reference; confirm at the pilot.
