# Flat vector

Contents: prompt fragment, fits, cautions

## Prompt fragment

Flat vector illustration: clean geometric shapes, solid color fills from a limited palette of six colors or fewer, uniform outline or no outline, no gradients, no texture, no shadow. Keep the character proportions and key colors of the reference. Centered, margin around the shape, transparent background, no frame, no extra letters.

## Fits well

- Characters built from simple shapes.
- Messenger stickers viewed small; high contrast reads well.
- Packs where props and objects matter (subject mix concepts).

## Cautions

- Gradients sneak in; repeat the no-gradient line at the end of the prompt.
- Large flat areas can show banding when printed; ask a proof.
- Tiny details of the reference vanish. Accept or choose original-match.
